## uQR

`callsign_badger/cb_uqr.py` is based on [uQR](https://github.com/JASchilz/uQR/blob/master/uQR.py).
Its BSD/MIT notices are in `callsign_badger/licenses/uQR.txt`.
Local changes add a CPython `re` fallback for `ure` and use a raw regex literal.

## Mona Sans

`callsign_badger/assets/MonaSans-SemiBold.af` was converted from
[Mona Sans SemiBold](https://github.com/github/mona-sans/blob/main/fonts/static/ttf/MonaSans-SemiBold.ttf)
to the Badgeware AF format, using printable ASCII glyphs.
The SIL Open Font License is included in `callsign_badger/licenses/MonaSans-OFL.txt`.
